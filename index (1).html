Trace My T-Shirt

You are the new sourcing manager at Threadline, a clothing brand launching a basic cotton T-shirt. You'll make 100,000 shirts and sell them to stores for $12 each. Your job is to pick a supplier for each step, from the cotton field to the store shelf.

Every supplier tells you its price, carbon, and water use. What it doesn't tell you is the problem it might be hiding.

5 stepsCotton farm, spinning mill, dye house, sewing factory, and shipping. Choose one supplier for each.
3 auditsAn audit costs $5,000 and shows you what a supplier is really like. You can't check everyone, so choose wisely.
1 news reportAfter launch, reporters investigate your supply chain. Anything hidden will come out.
